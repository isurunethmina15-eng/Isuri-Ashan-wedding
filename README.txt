ISURI & ASHAN — BILINGUAL WEDDING INVITATION
=============================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview in your browser.
4. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and the Google Maps link.

LANGUAGE
Use the English / සිංහල buttons at the top to switch the page language.

EDIT THESE DETAILS
- Couple names and wording: index.html
- Wedding date and countdown: index.html and js/script.js
- Venue and Google Maps link: index.html
- Colours, spacing and layout: css/style.css
- Background photo: replace images/background.jpg with your own image.
- Gallery photos: replace the three photo-placeholder blocks in index.html with <img> elements and your own image files.
- RSVP: replace the sample WhatsApp link in index.html with your own RSVP link or phone number.

NOTES
- The date currently uses 25 January 2027, as discussed earlier.
- The ceremony time is intentionally marked for updating.
- The photo gallery currently uses decorative placeholders.
- The background is designed to work with images/background.jpg; if no image is present, the CSS gradient remains as a fallback.
- Add your music file as music/wedding-music.mp3. The floating Play music / Mute music button works on mobile and desktop. Music starts only after a guest taps the button because browsers commonly block autoplay.
- The music and video folders are included for your own media files.
